Codync Cloud runs a Linux computer for your bots on your own account with a cloud provider: **Boat** (in the EU) or **InstaBox** (in the US). Use it when no computer of yours stays on, or to give bots a machine of their own. Codync charges nothing for it; the provider bills your account under its own plan. Codync Cloud is a **beta** feature.

It needs a free Codync account (Apple, Google or GitHub). Every app reaches a cloud computer like any other computer in your account: the iPhone and Android apps, and the desktop app while you're using its window (so the cloud computer can still sleep when you aren't).

## Start a cloud computer

1. Make an API key at your provider. In Codync, **How to make a key** on the provider's key sheet shows the steps and links to the provider's page.
2. Open **Computers**, and in the **Codync Cloud** section choose **+**.
3. Name the cloud computer, pick the provider, and paste its key when asked. The key is checked with the provider before it's saved.
4. Choose **Create**. It shows "Preparing your cloud computer…" for a minute or two, then appears under its name with its own Codync bot.

Each account can have up to three cloud computers, on either provider. A provider's key stays while a cloud computer runs there; replace it from the provider's key sheet (or the computer's **⋯** menu), and remove it once no cloud computer uses it.

## Sleep and wake

A cloud computer goes to sleep while its bots have nothing to do, so it doesn't run up your provider's bill. It shows "Sleeping · wakes when you message", and it wakes when you message one of its bots, when a routine is due or when a webhook arrives. Waking takes a few seconds, longer after a long sleep.

## Your keys

- **The provider key** is stored encrypted, only for your account, and never shown again: the apps show its last four characters.
- **On the cloud computer**, your bots and their agents run as a user without sudo. API keys you save for agents and for voice go through a keeper: the bots can use them but not read them. Your agents' own sign-ins (Claude Code's or Codex's login) stay readable to those agents, as on any computer.

## Rename, snapshots and delete

From the cloud computer's **⋯** menu:

- **Rename…** changes its name in every app.
- **Delete snapshots** erases the earlier states the provider keeps of the computer (where it keeps any), while it's awake.
- **Delete cloud computer** deletes the machine at the provider, with its bots and files.
